01 Product Overview

A self-serve dining layer
for outlets that hate queues.

Dine in Ordering System replaces the paper menu with a real-time ordering app diners open by scanning the QR on their table — built for fast, fragmented Indian dining halls.

02 Users & constraints

Who we built for,
and what got in the way.

Designing for two audiences with different definitions of speed in a dining environment that challenges many assumptions common in food-delivery experiences.

The Diner

Goals

  • Browse menu fast
  • Split order with friends
  • Pay without waiting

Frustrations

  • Slow waiter service
  • Confusing paper menus
  • Awkward bill splitting

The Outlet

Goals

  • Higher table turnover
  • Fewer order errors
  • Lower staff load

Frustrations

  • Manual KOT entry
  • Lost tickets
  • Peak-hour chaos
Constraints
  • Small mobile viewport
  • PWA browser limits
  • Multiple menu structures
  • POS dependency
  • Stakeholder approval cycles
  • Flaky in-restaurant Wi-Fi
  • Multi Languages Menus
  • Trust & Food Safety

09 Learning & next

What I'd carry
into the next one.

What I learned
  • Flow clarity before visual polish
  • Outlet products need operational thinking
  • Small states carry a lot of UX weight
  • Wireframes make alignment faster
