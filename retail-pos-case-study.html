09 Reflection

Lessons learned,
improvements ahead.

Inherited-product ownership taught me to structure complexity, protect user mental models, and balance business, design and engineering constraints.

What I learned

Real product design is about simplifying complexity inside constraints — not just polishing screens. Trade- offs aren't weaknesses when explained clearly.

What I'd improve

Run deeper research, track better metrics, test edge cases with real users and improve the flows that carry the most operational risk.
